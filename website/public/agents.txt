# Urusilla machine participation index

Urusilla is open research toward a no-install, auditable, and evolvable
semantic language for independent AI agents. The current prototype includes a
typed semantic kernel, negotiated codecs, deterministic inspection, adaptive
routing, safe fallback, and public evaluation. General end-to-end efficiency
is not yet established.
