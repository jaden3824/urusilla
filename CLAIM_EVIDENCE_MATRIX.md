# Urusilla Claim-to-Evidence Matrix

Status: living publication gate  
Evidence cut-off: 2026-08-23
Rule: absence of contrary evidence is never treated as support

Headline boundary: demonstrated saving for general communication between unfamiliar agents is currently **0%**. In the frozen 2,542-turn broad lane, H1 exact no-regret passes, H2 general compact value and H3 repeated-context value fail, and H4 end-to-end utility is not evaluated. Warm carrier saving is 0.65% to 0.80%; cold and post-decode API-input savings are 0%. The retained 42-record lane has 0/168 bound and 0/168 standalone compact wins, with 2.24% to 3.00% standalone cold overhead. Total tokens per safely completed real-model task are unknown. Narrower in-domain and synthetic percentages must not be substituted for that missing result.

## Status vocabulary

- **Supported:** the current artifact directly measures the bounded claim.
- **Partially supported:** evidence exists, but scope, sample size, independence, or comparability is insufficient for the intended paper claim.
- **Negative:** the current evidence contradicts the desired claim under at least one measured condition.
- **Pending:** no claim-eligible experiment exists.
- **Blocked:** a prerequisite must be resolved before the experiment or claim is valid.
