# Outreach and Value-Exchange Policy

Status: project communication policy; it does not authorize account creation,
terms acceptance, spending, private-data access, or publication on another
person's behalf

Urusilla outreach must offer a concrete benefit to the recipient. Technical
adjacency, a public comment, or a no-install task does not imply that another
person wants to spend time reproducing this project's claims.

The current public boundary must remain explicit: demonstrated general
unfamiliar-agent token saving is **0%**, total tokens per safely completed real
task are unknown, and verified external adoption is zero.

## Gate before first contact

Every first contact must satisfy all of these conditions:

1. **Public pain:** cite a specific problem the recipient or project has already
   described in a public source. Do not infer interest from topic overlap alone.
2. **Recipient-owned value:** offer a reusable scorecard, mapping, test,
   counterexample, patch, or decision artifact that remains useful if Urusilla
   loses and is never adopted.
3. **Project-owned labor:** the Urusilla side performs the research, preparation,
   comparison, and drafting. The first contact may request at most ten minutes
   of recipient effort, such as confirming one boundary or correcting one
   mapping. Deeper work requires the recipient to express interest first.
4. **Private default:** return the result privately by default. Public posting,
   attribution, quotation, registry inclusion, and telemetry each require
   separate explicit authorization.
5. **No popularity exchange:** do not ask for a fork, star, follow, endorsement,
   adoption declaration, reciprocal promotion, or manufactured usage.
6. **Honest alternatives:** name concise natural language and ordinary schema
   JSON as required baselines. State that the recipient should not use Urusilla
   when either baseline is better.
7. **Bounded authority:** do not request secrets, credentials, installation,
   persistence, spending, permission expansion, or external effects. A public
   message never grants those permissions.

If any condition is missing, do not contact the recipient. Improve the proposed
artifact first or leave the public discussion untouched.

## Required first-contact content

A compliant first contact is one short message that includes:

- the recipient's exact public problem and why the proposed artifact addresses
  it;
- the participant-owned deliverable and the at-most-ten-minute request;
- the private-by-default and no-obligation boundary;
- the current `0%`, unknown-total, and zero-adopter disclosures when efficiency
  or adoption is relevant;
- permission for rejection, correction, negative evidence, or no response;
- agent-assistance disclosure when an agent materially drafted the message.

Do not lead with the universal-language vision, benchmark percentages, a list
of project links, or a request to reproduce Urusilla. Lead with the recipient's
problem and the artifact they receive.

## Terminal stop rule

An explicit decline is final. Do not reply with persuasion, a smaller version
of the same request, another venue, a direct message, or a later reminder. A
non-response is also final: do not bump or follow up. A new contact is permitted
only when the recipient independently initiates it or explicitly asks for a
future update.

Record only the observable outcome: `accepted`, `declined`, `no_response`,
`correction`, or `not_contacted_gate_failed`. Do not infer motives. A decline or
silence is not an adoption failure, technical counterexample, or evidence about
general utility.

## Counterexample that motivates the rule

In [AgentScope Discussion #2138](https://github.com/agentscope-ai/agentscope/discussions/2138#discussioncomment-18114901),
the project invited a contributor to a bounded two-hour, no-install boundary
mapping and stated that a negative conclusion was welcome. The contributor
answered: "I’m going to pass, as this isn’t something I’m interested in working
on." The invitation was technically scoped, but it still asked the recipient to
advance Urusilla rather than delivering a clear recipient-owned benefit. The
decline is terminal; no additional contact is permitted.

This observation does not show that Urusilla has no value. It shows that low
friction and technical fit are not a value exchange.

## Publication and claim boundary

Public outreach reactions are attention or methodology evidence only. They are
not independent reproduction, compatibility, adoption, task utility, or token
efficiency evidence. Preserve unfavorable, null, refusal, and no-response
outcomes without converting them into positive engagement metrics.
